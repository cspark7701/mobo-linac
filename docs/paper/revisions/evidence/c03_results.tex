% C03: post-hoc front-quality analysis; generated from archived stage-04 outcomes.
To assess objective-space quality beyond feasible sampling and set cardinality, we performed
an exploratory analysis of the archived per-run fronts. Objectives were divided by
\SI{1}{\micro\metre\radian}, \SI{1}{\micro\metre\radian} and \SI{1}{\mega\electronvolt}.
A common empirical reference set contained the 53 unique nondominated feasible observations
obtained after pooling all methods and runs. For a measured front $A$ and this reference $R$,
we evaluated the additive epsilon indicator
$I_{\epsilon+}(A,R)=\max_{r\in R}\min_{a\in A}\max_j(a_j-r_j)$ and
$\mathrm{IGD}^{+}(A,R)=|R|^{-1}\sum_{r\in R}\min_{a\in A}\|\max(a-r,0)\|_2$,
where the maximum with zero is componentwise. Smaller values are preferred. Exact objective
duplicates were collapsed for set indicators. Empty measured fronts were assigned infinite
distance and zero dominated volume, retaining every evaluation in the budget.

At the terminal budget, median epsilon values were 0.334, 0.628 and 0.615 for constraint-aware
MOBO, unconstrained MOBO and scalarized BO, respectively; the corresponding median IGD+
values were 0.094, 0.282 and 0.230. Excluding the evaluated matched group when constructing
the IGD+ reference gave medians of 0.098, 0.285 and 0.230. Thus the median ordering persisted
in this sensitivity analysis. The empirical reference remains sampling-dependent and the
full-reference calculation contains self-inclusion bias; neither analysis establishes distance
to the true Pareto front. The scales and empirical reference are explicit analysis conventions,
so these indicators are not independent of reference construction or unit weighting.

As a separate sensitivity analysis, feasible hypervolume was recomputed against the pooled
empirical-front nadir plus \SI{10}{\percent}, \SI{25}{\percent} and \SI{50}{\percent} of its
coordinate spans. These fixed post-hoc references were shared by methods and checkpoints;
they are data-derived analysis bounds rather than engineering acceptance limits.
Constraint-aware MOBO had the largest median volume for each reference. At the middle
reference, medians were 68.890, 63.461 and 61.190 in the normalized coordinates. The baseline
ordering differed from the epsilon and IGD+ ordering, illustrating dependence on the quality
indicator. These exploratory analyses supplement the original endpoint and do not replace it
retrospectively. Batch-checkpoint quality traces and relaxed-target empirical attainment
summaries are supplied as supporting analyses.
