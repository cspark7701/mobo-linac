# C04 exploratory paired-inference protocol

Specified before generating C04 results. Use the C03 archived-profile run table and the ten matched initial-design groups; no simulation rows are independent inference units.

- Four terminal endpoints: feasible fraction, additive epsilon, IGD+ and the middle exploratory HV reference (+25% pooled-front span). These are fixed before calculating tests. All three pairwise method comparisons form one family of twelve two-sided tests. The other HV references and leave-group-out IGD+ remain descriptive sensitivity checks, not an opportunity to choose the smallest p-value.
- Effect size is the arithmetic mean within-group difference (first listed method minus second). Report a paired percentile bootstrap interval with 10,000 resamples of the ten complete group identities, jointly resampling every method/endpoint, using a fixed analysis RNG. Intervals are pointwise exploratory intervals, not simultaneous confidence regions, and ten groups limit precision.
- Use an exact signed-rank test: discard exact zero differences, assign midranks to tied absolute differences, and enumerate all signs of the remaining ranks. The two-sided p-value is the fraction of sign assignments whose absolute signed-rank sum is at least the observed absolute sum. All-zero differences give p=1. Validity assumes independent matched groups and a sign-symmetric null distribution of differences; the methods were not randomly relabeled. Apply Holm correction over the full twelve-test family at alpha=0.05. Do not test the original all-zero HV endpoint.
- Audit initialization directly: count feasible observations and first-feasible evaluation in the common initial 16; distinguish acquisition-phase feasibility in evaluations 17–160. Report all ten groups, including groups already feasible initially. Do not select an initially-infeasible subgroup for an unbiased ranking claim.
- Audit IDs, exact shared initial design, objectives and labels through the C03 loader. Bootstrap analysis RNG is unrelated to simulation seeds. Keep seed mappings in the evidence file, not the manuscript prose.
- All inference is post hoc/exploratory and conditional on the archived acceptance profile and metric choices. Corrected p-values do not remove metric-selection or model assumptions. New physical performance claims about revised methods remain outside this task.
