# Frozen round6 validation protocol

## Authorization and scope

The author adopted numerical validation and matched raw/normalized campaigns on 31 October 2026 and excluded constrained scalarization. The global ceiling is two CPU evaluation workers; numerical libraries use one thread per process. GPU modelling uses CUDA and float64. Execute studies serially, with a shared run lock; no other study from this chain may consume evaluation workers simultaneously.

This protocol is frozen before new physical outcomes. The machine-readable companion is `r6_specification.json`; selected identities and inclusion probabilities are in `r6_sampling_manifest.csv`, including original controls, and all 240 candidate strata, including empty strata, are in `r6_sampling_strata.csv`. Inputs are hashed. These studies test setting-dependent numerical sensitivity and a particular residual/temperature convention, not machine reliability or globally optimal fronts.

## Representative numerical sample

Population: all 4,800 original primary observations, three methods and ten matched groups. All are simulation-valid. Cross method, group, original feasibility, search stage (evaluations 1–80 / 81–160), and distance to the nearest diagnostic boundary (absolute normalized margin at most 0.1 / greater than 0.1). Upper margins are (limit−value)/scale, lower margins (value−limit)/scale; transverse/bunch scales are 0.001 in SI units, energy sides 5 MeV, transmission 0.1. Near-boundary includes either side. Record nearest channel and minimum signed margin for each observation; report channel-specific coverage without claiming equal allocation by channel.

Independent simple random sampling without replacement selects two settings per nonempty stratum, or its census when smaller. The frozen seed and sorted population produce 387 settings: 132 constrained, 129 unconstrained and 126 scalarized. Inclusion probability is n/N, positive for every historical observation. Shared-initialization identities remain separate method-population units. No sample is selected using a new outcome. Strata with one observation are censuses; there are no sampled singleton strata with an unestimable within-stratum variance.

The allocation aims at approximately 130 settings per method, retaining every group and both feasibility states/stages when present. An unstratified independent binary fraction with n=130 has worst-case standard error about 0.044 and nominal 95% half-width about 0.086 before finite-population correction. This is a planning scale, not a promised precision: unequal weights and group dependence can widen intervals. The sample can leave small method differences unresolved. No expansion or early stopping based on direction/significance is permitted.

Run every selected control vector at five levels (1,935 attempts): production; fixed-source medium (48-cell grids, half step); fixed-source fine (64-cell grids, quarter step); combined medium (20k reconstructed quasi-random particles, medium grids/step); combined fine (40k particles, fine grids/step). Fixed-source contrasts hold the actual production file unchanged. Combined-source contrasts change particle sampling and cannot isolate pure discretization. Reconstructed sources reproduce marginals, not a verified full initial distribution. Preserve generated decks, actual file hashes and generator identity.

First execute nominal reproduction for the entire sample and require agreement with historical diagnostics/objectives (relative tolerance 1e-9, absolute tolerance 1e-12). A discrepancy or invalid nominal run blocks refinement pending investigation, with the failed attempt retained. No automatic physics changes or retries. Then execute remaining levels; retain invalid outcomes in every denominator and report failure rates separately.

Compare fixed fine to fixed medium and combined fine to combined medium. Diagnostic tolerances: 1% of each transverse/bunch limit, 0.01 MeV energy, 0.0001 transmission; objective relative tolerance 1%. These are declared diagnostic adequacy thresholds, not demonstrated error bounds. Report all crossings, shifts and failures; stable feasibility alone does not establish objective convergence. Failure of a tolerance is an adverse or unresolved result, not authorization for unbounded refinement.

Estimate each historical run's refined feasible fraction as sum_h N_h * sample_mean_h / 160. Use finite-population SRS variance sum_h (N_h/160)^2 (1−n_h/N_h) s_h^2/n_h. For census singleton strata the design variance is zero. Report conditional design uncertainty and paired-group uncertainty separately: ten-group bootstrap with 10,000 resamples, same resampled groups across methods, plus the design-based standard errors. Do not present bootstrap-only intervals as including full sample-selection uncertainty. For pair differences use a conservative sum of the two design standard errors if dependence cannot be established. Invalid refined observations count as not feasible for the attempt-inclusive endpoint; additionally bound the unknown physical feasibility by assigning all such observations first false then true. Distinguish sampled flip rates, weighted estimates and directly measured full-population totals. Boundary-channel summaries are descriptive and include sparse/absent categories.

## Matched residual-scaling experiment

Ten groups (42–51), two contemporaneous constrained qLogNEHVI arms, 160 ASTRA attempts per arm/group: 16 initial Sobol controls and 18 joint batches of eight (3,200 total). Start each pair from identical Sobol designs. Use the production configuration, source and nominal resolution, the same model family/fitting and 128 posterior samples, pruning, 1,024 raw starts, ten restarts, 200 iterations and recorded fallback policy. The historical raw campaigns are contextual, not substitutes for these controls. Run one campaign at a time, alternating raw-first/normalized-first by group parity. Hardware resource policy is identical; elapsed times are descriptive and subject to shared-host load.

Raw residuals retain physical units and temperature 0.001. Normalized residuals divide by the physical threshold scales above (two energy sides separately) with common dimensionless temperature 0.001. Thus physical smoothing scales are 1 micrometre, 1 microradian, 1 micrometre bunch length, 5 keV on either energy side, and 0.0001 transmission. Raw physical scales are 1 millimetre, 1 milliradian, 1 millimetre, 0.001 eV and 0.001 transmission. This changes relative smoothing widths, not just units; no width-preserving third arm is adopted and no pure normalization effect will be claimed.

Initial Sobol seed is the group. Independent deterministic per-iteration fitting, posterior and proposal streams use the integer formulas in the specification, identical across arms within a group. Export these identities and convention metadata; do not promise historical bitwise replay. Record explicit mode/scales/temperature, sampler count, pruning and all fallback outcomes in new configuration/proposal artifacts and checkpoints. Physical diagnostic observations and feasibility tests remain unchanged. Mock tests/pilots precede physical execution; no extra physical tuning campaign is allocated.

Primary endpoint: paired normalized-minus-raw all-attempt feasible fraction (denominator 160); the post-initialization fraction (144) is a prespecified supporting endpoint. Report all ten group differences, means and paired percentile bootstrap intervals (10,000 resamples, fixed analysis seed), noting small-sample uncertainty. Report invalid attempts, channel-group violations, search-stage effects and elapsed cost. No confirmatory front-quality endpoint is added; any objective-space analysis is exploratory with its reference construction disclosed. Do not select a temperature, seed or subset after viewing outcomes. A negative or inconclusive result is valid.

## Resource budget, provenance and stopping

The total physical budget is **5,135 attempts**, without automatic scientific retries or optional comparator. Per-tracking timeout is 24 hours for refinement; scaling retains the production timeout and zero retries. Incomplete interrupted attempts are preserved and accounted for before any explicitly recorded recovery attempt; they are not silently deleted or overwritten. Completed task checksums and exact plan identities gate resumption.

Measured historical primary mean tracking time is 148.7 seconds; refinement production/combined-medium/combined-fine means were 206.7/1,176.4/2,614.4 seconds on the earlier concurrent host. Using the combined levels as a conservative proxy for fixed-source medium/fine gives about 418 hours of two-worker tracking for the refinement sample. Scaling contributes about 66 hours of tracking plus model/acquisition time. Plan approximately **three weeks**, with a broad **two-to-four-week** shared-host range, not a deadline; two-worker runtimes may differ substantially from the prior high-concurrency measurements. Record measured pilot/first-stage rates without changing allocations based on outcome. This replaces the preliminary chat estimate before the full level-cost calculation.

Historical disk usage is approximately 30 GB for 4,800 primary evaluations and 6.4 GB for 150 earlier refinement attempts. Proportional estimates are about 83 GB refinement and 20 GB scaling, with a 200 GB allowance including models and logs. Available disk at freeze was about 895 GiB. Require at least 100 GiB free at stage boundaries; pause and preserve evidence if unavailable. No automatic pruning of raw evidence.

New outputs only under `results/revision_round6/`. Before launching, freeze and commit implementation and protocol; record git commit, environment, input hashes and full commands. Preserve all historical records. Do not claim completed convergence, normalized-method superiority or reviewer acceptance before the analysis. Push the related revisions only after the adopted tasks and final validation are finished, as the author requested.
