# C10 — Design-specific robustness interpretation

## Status
Completed for the retained lattice-jitter scope. The expanded RF-amplitude/JKPS/alignment model and tolerance scan are explicitly not adopted in this revision; no broad machine-reliability or baseline-superiority claim is made. This is a reasoned response, not a claim that the additional runs were performed or accepted by the reviewer.

## Reproduction and checks
Run `python scripts/analyze_revision_robustness.py` in the production environment. The script reads the primary stage-06 and survey stage-05 request/result records, independently recomputes all objective statistics, feasibility, threshold-normalized margins and composite scores, and checks agreement to floating-point precision. It checks all 600 ASTRA input hashes and 1,800 Xemit/Yemit/Zemit output hashes against their attempt manifests. c10_metadata.json records source hashes and the full saved error specification. Original summaries and simulations are unchanged.

## Statistics and identity
Each audit contains six distinct nominal representatives and 300 distinct perturbed parameter vectors, partitioned into six groups of 50. Exact run/evaluation identities are in c10_design_statistics.csv. The primary selection uses the first prescribed primary run, independently of robustness outcomes. Channel draws are Gaussian and independent across channels and sample index; phase controls impose perfectly correlated errors within each cavity pair. Saved deltas are exactly reused across all six designs (common random numbers). Do not pool heterogeneous designs or treat all 300 results as independent trials of one design.

All twelve design-specific groups have 50/50 feasible outcomes. Their two-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson interval is [0.9288782635, 1]; their one-sided 95% failure upper bound is 0.0581550791. These are conditional frequentist bounds under the specified sampling model, not confidence in actual machine reliability or simultaneous bounds for all designs. Objective means, population standard deviations (ddof=0, matching the archived estimator), empirical percentiles and mean ratios are separately tabulated. Percentiles are empirical distributions, not confidence limits on means.

## Margins and the historical score
For upper/lower bounds use (u-y)/abs(u) and (y-l)/abs(l). Take minima over trials and channels. Energy lower/upper denominators are 195 and 205 MeV. The separate c10_constraint_margins.csv preserves physical-unit margins and, for energy only, half-band normalized values using 5 MeV. A different cross-channel scale changes ranking, so the threshold convention is not a universal robustness metric. The minimum primary normalized margin 0.01594871795 corresponds to 3.11 MeV lower-bound headroom, i.e. 0.622 half-bands.

Independent score recomputation uses S=P*max(0.5,1+mu)/max(1,D). Primary min_sigma_energy and balanced have exactly equal S=1.0191219512195122 because both D values are below one and both upper-energy minima are identical at recorded precision. min_emit_y (0.9592204135667707) and crowding_distance_max (0.9592350587326485) differ at full precision and only agree after rounding to four decimals. The floor of 0.5 is inactive for these feasible trials. This is expected clipping/rounding behavior, not evidence of a scoring implementation bug. The revised paper reports P, uncertainty, D and margins instead of presenting S as a physical ranking; the original score is preserved.

## Error budget and non-adopted extensions
The performed protocol uses 1x nominal RMS amplitudes: 0.1% relative standard deviation for each magnet control and 0.1 degree for each gun/structure-pair phase control. These are standard deviations, not hard limits. No RF-amplitude, position/rotation alignment or source perturbation was physically applied. Requested 1% source scales are explicitly recorded as unsupported by the fixed source model.

Reviewer 2's prose suggests multipliers 0.1/0.2/0.5 whereas the associated task request can be read as absolute phase amplitudes. No scan has been run and neither interpretation is silently adopted. Any future scan must state separate physical RMS values for every channel and an unambiguous dimensionless multiplier relative to this baseline, plus phase correlations, mapping checks and full validation. The present paper limits claims to the performed 1x lattice-only protocol rather than inventing a resolution of a scan it does not perform. The omitted broader model prevents direct reliability comparison with JKPS and prevents comparative robustness claims against the other BO methods. Larger samples would tighten per-design bounds but cannot repair missing error channels; no machine reliability target is asserted from these data.
