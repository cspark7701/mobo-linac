% C04: run-level paired inference conditional on the C03 analysis conventions.
Five of the ten shared initial designs already contained a feasible setting. First-feasible
counts within those initial designs therefore cannot be credited to an acquisition function.
Using only the subsequent 144 evaluations, while retaining all ten groups, median feasible
fractions were \SI{45.1}{\percent}, \SI{16.3}{\percent} and \SI{13.2}{\percent} for
constraint-aware MOBO, unconstrained MOBO and scalarized BO, respectively.

Exploratory inference used the ten matched groups as the sampling units. Paired bootstrap
intervals resampled complete groups jointly across methods, with 10,000 resamples.
We applied exact two-sided signed-rank tests~\cite{Wilcoxon1945} to four terminal endpoints---feasible fraction,
additive epsilon, IGD+ and the middle post-hoc hypervolume reference---and used Holm correction~\cite{Holm1979}
across all twelve pairwise comparisons. Zero differences were excluded from the signed ranks,
tied absolute differences received midranks, and all sign assignments were enumerated.
The tests assume independent groups and sign-symmetric differences under the null; the
pointwise bootstrap intervals are conditional on the fixed empirical reference and are not
multiplicity-adjusted.

Mean paired feasible-fraction differences for constraint-aware MOBO relative to unconstrained
and scalarized BO were 0.248 (\SI{95}{\percent} interval 0.206--0.293) and
0.280 (0.249--0.313), respectively; both Holm-adjusted $p$-values were 0.0234.
For epsilon and IGD+, comparisons with scalarized BO had adjusted $p$-values of 0.0234 and
0.0410, whereas comparisons with unconstrained MOBO had adjusted $p$-values of 0.0586 and
0.0977. At the middle hypervolume reference, mean paired differences were 5.202 (3.062--7.441)
and 7.104 (5.132--9.315), with adjusted $p$-values of 0.0313 and 0.0234. None of the four
baseline-to-baseline comparisons passed the specified 0.05 threshold after correction.
These post-hoc results support a feasible-sampling advantage under the archived profile;
objective-quality evidence depends on the endpoint and comparator and does not establish
uniform algorithm superiority or global optimality.
