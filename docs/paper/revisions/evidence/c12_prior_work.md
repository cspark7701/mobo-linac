## Completed revision
The Introduction now explicitly defines the paper as a comparison of constraint-aware MOBO, unconstrained MOBO and scalarized BO. It cites the completed MOGA and error-analysis studies, distinguishes the published NAPAC paper from ICABU presentations, and excludes fresh MOGA/NSGA-II optimization or published-setting reevaluation. Both reviewer response drafts record that scope decision without implying reviewer agreement.

## Verified comparison
The JINST abstract's summary (3.79 micrometre radian, 0.30737 MeV) differs from its section 4.3 tracked example (3.9586/3.9424 micrometre radian, 0.165% at 201.79 MeV). Multiplying the latter fraction by mean energy gives about 0.3330 MeV. The manuscript identifies both rather than treating the abstract as the specific tracked setting. It describes the current knee as a transverse/longitudinal trade-off, not a matched algorithm advantage. Prior source specifications do not establish identical initial distributions.

NAPAC WECN03 describes eight controls with independent cavity phases, fractional energy spread, different feasibility limits and a multiweight scalarized comparison. The present six-control paired-cavity/absolute-spread problem is different. C01 supplies the separate acceptance reclassification; C02 and C09 document the phase convention and resolution correspondence limits.
