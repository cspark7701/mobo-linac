1. **No blanket rerun:** Existing campaigns and fixed-source nominal/jitter results are complete and reproducible. No failure recovery or additional optimization budget is required merely because the runs ended.
2. **Prioritize targeted physics convergence:** Re-evaluate selected audited Pareto settings and a baseline with appropriately regenerated higher-statistics particle distributions and coordinated space-charge-grid refinement, retaining charge and intended beam moments. Check objective stability, constraint margins, and ordering of candidate trade-offs against declared tolerances. Increasing cell count alone can reduce particles per cell; particle and grid choices must be studied together.
3. **Separate reference analysis from new simulations:** A clearly labeled supplementary reference-sensitivity or physical-objective analysis can use saved observations without ASTRA reruns. Preserve the predeclared zero-HV primary result. If a new confirmatory hypervolume comparison is desired, predeclare a scientifically justified reference/protocol and use fresh held-out seeds; do not present a reference selected after viewing these results as the original primary endpoint. More seeds with the same non-discriminating endpoint alone do not solve the issue.
4. **Full-chain robustness is conditional new work:** A verified source-generation mapping and actual simulations varying charge/laser size/duration are needed before making those robustness claims. The present 50 lattice samples per design do not establish a population failure probability of zero. These claims can instead remain explicitly excluded.
5. **Review constraint scaling before new BO:** The callbacks return native-unit residuals (metres, radians, eV, and transmission), while acquisition construction leaves BoTorch's common default smoothing temperature in use. This makes the smooth indicator unit-dependent. Audit dimensionless scaling or per-channel temperatures before another optimization study. A changed acquisition defines new campaigns; it must not silently relabel archived runs or alter their hard physical feasibility.
6. **Machine validation remains separate:** More ASTRA runs can strengthen numerical evidence but cannot establish agreement with accelerator measurements.
